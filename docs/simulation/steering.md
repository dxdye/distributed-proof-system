# Steering study (C-32)

Simulation of class-A review: 600 items, error rate 0.1, 40 honest reviewers and one attacker with s accounts; 50% of the erroneous items are the attacker's targets, which its accounts pass. The accounts gain trust by looking up planted errors (the worst case of C-07). τ: how much more likely an attacking account is drawn for a target. Identity weight: the attackers' rates shrunk towards 0.5 in the decision rule (1 = full weight). k_min = 3, δ_A = 0.001; pooled over seeds [0, 1, 2, 3, 4].
Generated by `pixi run dpc steering-study`.

Target success: targets accepted / targets. Baseline: the miss rate of the other erroneous items in the same runs, i.e. what honest misses alone explain.

## From the coordinator's limits to τ

τ = N·(1 − (1 − n/N)^m) for N open tickets, sets of n, and m sets seen per ticket the attacker takes (`dpc.sim.study.steering_factor`).

| m (sets seen per take) | τ (N = 200, n = 4) | τ (N = 1000, n = 4) | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4.0 | 4.0 | no rerolling: choosing alone |
| 2 | 7.9 | 8.0 | 1 unused set per take |
| 4 | 15.5 | 15.9 | 3 unused sets per take |
| 7 | 26.4 | 27.7 | 6 unused sets, 1 take a day |
| 25 | 79.3 | 95.3 | 24 sets a day, 1 take (before C-32) |
